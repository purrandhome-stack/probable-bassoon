Cat sitting services

Every visit includes feeding, fresh water, a litter tray clean and proper attention — not just a quick in-and-out. Here's what's on offer.

Drop-in visits

A 30, 45 or 60-minute visit for feeding, fresh water, a litter tray clean and playtime or a fuss, whichever your cat prefers that day. Good for a single day out or as part of a longer holiday plan.

from £12

Holiday care

Daily visits for the full length of your trip. You'll get a short update and a photo after each one, plus post, parcels and bins brought in so the house looks lived-in.

from £12/visit

Medication & extra care

Comfortable administering tablets, liquid medication or insulin injections (with your instructions and vet's guidance), and giving extra time to older or anxious cats.

+£3 per visit

Multi-cat households

Two, three or more cats under one roof — every visit covers the whole household, with a small additional charge per extra cat.

+£3 per cat

Meet & greet

A relaxed visit before any booking begins, so your cat can meet me first and I can take note of routines, favourite hiding spots and any quirks worth knowing.
