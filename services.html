Cat sitting services

Every visit includes feeding, fresh water, a litter tray clean and proper attention — not just a quick in-and-out. Here's what's on offer.

Our Services

Drop-In Visits

A 20, 30 or 45-minute visit for feeding, fresh water, a litter tray clean and playtime or a fuss, whichever your cat prefers that day.

from £10

Holiday Care

Daily visits for the full length of your trip, with a short update and a photo after each one, plus post and bins brought in.

Medication & Extra Care

Comfortable administering tablets, liquid medication or insulin injections, and giving extra time to older or anxious cats.

from £17
