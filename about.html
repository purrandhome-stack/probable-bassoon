Hello, I'm Charli

I set up Purr & Home because I think cats deserve better than a cattery cage when their owners go away.

I'm based in Chorley and visit cats across the town and the surrounding villages. Every booking starts with a free meet & greet, so your cat can get used to me before I'm ever left in charge — and so I can note down routines, favourite spots and anything else worth knowing.

I keep bookings small on purpose. That means the cat you meet at the meet & greet is the same person who turns up for every visit — never a rotating cast of strangers with a key to your house.
