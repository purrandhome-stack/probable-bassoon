Booking cat sitting over Christmas in Chorley

19 September 2026 · Purr & Home, Chorley

Christmas and New Year is the single busiest stretch of the year for cat sitters — more people travel, most trips overlap the same two weeks, and catteries and sitters alike fill up fast.

Why it's worth booking early

Because so many people are travelling on the same handful of dates, a sitter covering Chorley can only take on so many regular clients over the period before visits start clashing. Booking in October or November, rather than December, means a far better chance of getting the visit times you actually want.

What to think about before you book

  • Exact dates — including travel days either side, not just the days you're actually away.
  • Visit frequency — once a day is standard, but some households prefer two shorter visits, especially over a long break.
  • Who's around nearby — a neighbour or family member as a backup contact, in case of anything unexpected over a holiday period.
  • Any changes to routine — if visitors are staying at your home, or if your cat will be alone more or less than usual.

Meet & greets before the rush

If you know you'll need cover over Christmas, it's worth arranging the free meet & greet well before December — diaries get busy fast, and it's much easier to find a relaxed slot in autumn.
